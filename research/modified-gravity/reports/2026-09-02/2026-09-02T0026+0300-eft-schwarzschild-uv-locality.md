# All-orders Schwarzschild EFT corrections require UV locality

**Accepted:** 2026-09-02 00:26 +03:00  
**Classification:** theoretical consistency / gravitational EFT / higher-derivative gravity / UV locality / black-hole metrics  
**Source quality:** peer-reviewed primary literature — *Physical Review D* 114, 066002 (published 2026-09-01)  
**Significance:** high

## Development

Yang Liu, Alexey S. Koshelev, Anna Tokareva, and Ziyue Zhu derive dominant corrections to the Schwarzschild metric from an infinite higher-derivative gravitational EFT expansion and resum the leading asymptotic contributions into a closed form. Their central result is that the perturbative metric deformation is convergent only for UV-localizable theories. This ties the mathematical viability of an all-orders corrected black-hole metric directly to the high-energy behavior of graviton scattering amplitudes.

For non-localizable UV behavior, the corresponding form factors generate a metric-deformation series that diverges everywhere except at spatial infinity, so a formally written infinite tower of higher-derivative operators does not automatically define a usable perturbative geometry.

The paper also analytically continues the all-orders result to logarithmic form factors appearing in the one-loop effective action of four-dimensional gravity. In the asymptotic regime studied, these one-loop logarithmic corrections can dominate the tree-level EFT corrections when the latter admit a convergent resummation.

## Why this crosses the significance threshold

- The result is now peer-reviewed and published in *Physical Review D*.
- It supplies an **all-orders consistency condition** rather than another model-specific observable.
- It connects black-hole metric deformations to UV locality and graviton-amplitude growth, giving a theory-level falsification gate for higher-derivative modified-gravity constructions.
- The framework is directly relevant to any attempt to justify exotic or engineered metrics through infinite higher-curvature / higher-derivative EFT corrections.

## Relevance to Warp-bubble / metric-engineering research

For higher-derivative warp or exotic-spacetime models, background-level solutions are not enough. If support for an unusual metric relies on an infinite derivative expansion, the associated series must define a controlled geometry rather than a formal divergent deformation. This result therefore adds a new validation gate:

1. identify the EFT operator tower and form factor;
2. test the UV-locality/localizability assumptions;
3. verify convergence or controlled resummability of the metric deformation;
4. only then proceed to energy-condition, characteristic-cone, stability, and observable-signature tests.

This does not produce a warp solution, but it removes a class of formally attractive yet non-resummable metric constructions from the viable search space.

## Limits

- The explicit derivation focuses on Schwarzschild backgrounds and the asymptotic far-field regime.
- The analysis is perturbative in EFT coefficients and keeps the curvature expansion to the order treated in the paper.
- The closed-form resummation assumes form factors with the analytic structure required by the derivation; general nonanalytic completions need separate treatment.
- Extension to Kerr and genuinely time-dependent or exotic geometries remains future work.
