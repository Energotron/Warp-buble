## Significant result

The paper classifies the local parity-even pure-gravity nonmetricity theory through four derivatives. After canonicalisation and integration by parts, the nonlinear theory contains 116 independent operators, but around Minkowski spacetime only nine quadratic structures survive. Imposing unrestricted linearised diffeomorphism invariance leaves a three-parameter family.

The generic regular spectrum contains the usual massless graviton together with a massive spin-two state and a massive scalar. The crucial consistency result is that the massive spin-two pole necessarily carries residue opposite to the healthy massless graviton, i.e. it is a ghost at the linear level. The branch-complete classification leaves only GR/STEGR and a scalar extension with no four-derivative tensor term as fully healthy Minkowski loci. Therefore, within this finite local metric formulation, obtaining genuine four-derivative ultraviolet suppression in the tensor sector requires accepting the unhealthy massive spin-two pole.

## Why it matters

This is a structural no-free-lunch result for higher-derivative symmetric teleparallel gravity, rather than a parameter constraint on one example. It sharply narrows the healthy local theory space and shows that four-derivative tensor improvement cannot be obtained in this setup without introducing a ghost degree of freedom.

For Warp-buble / QuantDeus, this matters as a model-selection filter: nonmetricity-based or higher-derivative effective geometries should not be treated as automatically healthier alternatives to curvature-based modified gravity merely because they reorganise the geometric variables. Any proposal relying on local four-derivative tensor suppression in this class must explicitly confront the massive spin-two residue problem or move beyond the assumptions of a finite local metric theory / Minkowski free-spectrum analysis.
