# Gravity from Entropy: tensor hyperbolicity obstruction on generic FLRW backgrounds

- **Accepted:** 2026-09-07 23:46 +03:00
- **Classification:** theory / theoretical consistency / emergent gravity / cosmological perturbations / gravitational-wave propagation
- **Source quality:** primary arXiv preprint; technically explicit principal-symbol / characteristic analysis; not yet peer-reviewed
- **Primary source:** David S. Pereira, *Gravitational Waves and Matter Constraints in Gravity from Entropy*, arXiv:2609.03082v1 (2026-09-02), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03082

## Result

The paper analyzes the tensor (TT) principal symbol of the "Gravity from Entropy" (GfE) framework on FLRW backgrounds. In the vacuum sector, in a symmetry-inheriting single-multiplet sector, and for the algebraic perfect-fluid constitutive embedding used in low-energy GfE cosmology, a nonzero real tensor characteristic exists only when

`dot(H) = K/a^2`.

Away from that locus the temporal principal roots become complex. This is not merely a modified gravitational-wave speed: it is a high-frequency hyperbolicity obstruction, so generic FLRW backgrounds in the studied sectors do not possess a healthy real tensor propagation cone at strict-principal order.

The author also gives a rank argument showing that one foundational matter multiplet cannot supply the isotropic bivector response required to remove the obstruction under the stated symmetry assumptions. Consequently, previously discussed flat slow-roll and phantom-like GfE cosmological solutions fail the tensor principal-condition away from exact de Sitter evolution.

## Why this changes the picture

This is a model-selection result rather than a parameter constraint. Background-level agreement with Friedmann cosmology is insufficient for GfE: the tensor sector imposes a stronger consistency condition that excludes broad classes of otherwise admissible cosmological histories in the tested sectors.

For the modified-gravity/warp research program, the useful lesson is structural: any emergent or effective gravity model that reconstructs geometry from non-standard response operators must be checked at the level of the principal symbol/characteristic cone before background solutions, exotic metrics, or energy-condition improvements are treated as physically viable. A geometry that solves background equations but lacks a real hyperbolic tensor sector cannot serve as a credible propagation framework.

## Limits

- This is a first-version preprint and not peer-reviewed.
- The obstruction is established for the sectors and isotropy assumptions analyzed in the paper; it is not a theorem excluding every possible multi-multiplet or less symmetric extension of GfE.
- The result is not a direct warp-drive construction or no-go theorem for warp spacetimes.

## Status

**Accepted as significant theoretical-consistency evidence.** It is not a duplicate of the existing symmetric-teleparallel ghost result: that entry concerns the particle spectrum/residue structure of local four-derivative nonmetricity gravity, while this result concerns loss of real tensor characteristics on generic FLRW backgrounds in an entropy-derived gravity model.
