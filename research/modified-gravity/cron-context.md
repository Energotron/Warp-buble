## Accepted development
- *Physical Review D* 114, 064017 — *Tests of scalar polarizations with multimessenger events*
- Classification: observation / interpretation / multimessenger gravitational-wave test / scalar polarization / parametrized modified gravity
- Status: peer-reviewed published paper (2026-09-08); confidence high for the stated GW170817 PPE inference and constraint improvements, medium for modified-gravity interpretation because the waveform extension is phenomenological and Bayesian evidence still favors GR
- Core result: incorporating the electromagnetic polarization-angle constraint from the GW170817/GRB afterglow into an extended PPE analysis tightens the quadrupolar scalar-amplitude modification bound by roughly 60% and the tensor-amplitude modification bound by roughly 30%. The scalar-amplitude posterior can lie 2–3σ from zero inside the extended model, but Bayesian model comparison favors pure GR with reported `Δ log Z = 4.67 ± 0.32`; therefore the accepted significance is the new multimessenger geometry prior, not a modified-gravity detection claim.
